Privacy Policy (Koe Nippo / こえにっぽー)

Last updated: 2026-07-20 / Applies to: こえにっぽー (Koe Nippo), the App Store version (the "App")

1. Introduction

The App is a daily-log and lifelog app for capturing notes quickly by voice or keyboard and reviewing them over time. To respect your privacy to the fullest extent, every feature of the App — including audio recording and speech-to-text — runs entirely on your device, and no information is ever sent anywhere.

2. Information We Collect or Transmit

The App does not collect or transmit any personal information.

The App has no server. No information about you is sent to the developer or any third party. There is no account registration or login of any kind.

Information we never obtain: email address, phone number, postal address, location, date of birth, photos, contacts, device identifiers, advertising IDs, or any other information that directly identifies you.

3. Microphone and Speech Recognition

  • Voice capture uses your device's microphone and iOS on-device speech recognition (SpeechAnalyzer). Neither your audio nor the recognized text is ever sent to an external server. All recognition happens on your device.
  • The speech-recognition language model is managed by iOS (an Apple system feature) and may be downloaded from Apple by the operating system, for example on first use. Even then, your audio and notes are never transmitted.
  • You can revoke microphone and speech-recognition permission at any time in the iOS Settings app. The App remains fully usable with keyboard input.

4. Information Stored on Your Device

The App stores the following information only on your device, solely to manage your notes. It is never sent outside the App.

CategoryDetailsPurpose
NotesNote text, creation timestamps, tags, daily one-line memosTimeline, calendar, and reflection views

This data lives in the App's sandbox on your device and cannot be accessed by other apps or external parties. If you use iOS device backups (such as iCloud Backup), on-device data may be included in your backup; this is standard iOS behavior, and the developer has no access to its contents.

5. Export

The App can export your notes as Markdown, plain text, CSV, or JSON. Export happens only when you explicitly initiate it, and you choose the destination via the iOS share sheet. The App never sends your notes anywhere automatically.

6. Third Parties and Tracking

  • The App performs no network communication whatsoever. It contains no advertising, analytics, or tracking of any kind.
  • Fonts are bundled with the App; nothing is fetched from external services (such as Google Fonts) to render the UI.
  • Your information is never shared with or sold to third parties.

7. Reviewing and Deleting Your Data

  • You can edit or delete individual notes at any time within the App. Draft audio is deleted when the note is finalized or deleted.
  • All data stored on your device is permanently erased when you delete (uninstall) the App.

8. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be revised as features or practices change. Significant changes will be communicated through App updates.
